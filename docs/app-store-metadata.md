## Description (≤4000)

### EN
TubeTrainer turns the scattered coaching you already follow on YouTube into your personal visual exercise manual — wrapped around a seriously fast gym tracker.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
• Attach the exact video — or the exact moment — to any exercise.
• Search YouTube or paste any link, even a Short.
• Prioritise the trainers you trust as favourite coaches.
• Compliant embedded playback with a clear "Open in YouTube".

A REAL TRACKER
• Log a set in seconds, one hand, with last-time values pre-filled.
• A rest timer that starts itself and notifies you — even with the app closed.
• Honest personal records: heaviest set, estimated 1RM, best volume.
• 50+ built-in exercises plus your own custom movements.
• Works offline — if a video won't load, logging never blocks.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No server. No analytics. Everything you log lives on your device and only leaves it if you export a backup yourself.

Free. iPhone. iOS 18+. Not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
